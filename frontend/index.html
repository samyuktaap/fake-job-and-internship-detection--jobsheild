Threshold Selection Rationale

Due to the severe class imbalance in real-world recruitment data (only 4.5% of postings are fraudulent), a naive 0.50 threshold with balanced weights yielded a false positive rate of 4.2% (96 false positives on validation).

Validation Default (0.50): Precision 50.0% | Recall 90.6% | F1 0.644
Validation Tuned (0.70): Precision 71.7% | Recall 81.1% | F1 0.761

By scanning candidate thresholds on the validation set only, threshold 0.70 maximized fraud F1-score while preserving ~80% fraud recall, slashing false accusations on the held-out test set from 96 down to 25.

Transparent Error Analysis: What Makes a False Positive or False Negative?

As required by the JobShield guidelines: no responsible model promises 100% accuracy. Examining failures reveals the structural limitations of tabular and text-based fraud detection.

False Positives (Legitimate Flagged as Fraud)

Why does the model flag legitimate listings?

Case 1: Commission-Only Remote Sales

Small sales agencies advertising 100% commission roles without a detailed company profile or corporate logo resemble work-from-home scams.

Case 2: Early-Stage Startups

Founders hiring informal contract interns using brief job descriptions and missing formal education requirements trigger low-legitimacy heuristics.

False Negatives (Scams Missed by Model)

How do sophisticated scams evade detection?

Case 1: Plagiarized Enterprise Postings

Scammers copy-pasted authentic job descriptions verbatim from Fortune 500 websites (e.g. Oracle, IBM), withholding the upfront fee demand until the email interview stage.

Case 2: Subtle Professional Phishing

Postings that avoid explicit trigger keywords like "wire transfer" and instead request standard resume documents that redirect to spoofed login portals.

Methodology & Group Allocation (JobShield Work Plan)

JobShield follows an audited five-member lifecycle designed for honest generalization, strict leakage prevention, and transparent error analysis.

Role Primary Ownership Key Deliverables Delivered in JobShield
1 – Data Lead Dataset research & hygiene EMSCAD deduplication (removed 2,031 duplicate templates to prevent train-test contamination); 70/15/15 stratified split.
2 – NLP Lead Preprocessing & TF-IDF Scikit-learn pipeline with text cleaning, unigram/bigram sublinear TF-IDF, and 10 domain risk indicator extractors.
3 – ML Lead Model training & tuning Baseline comparisons (Dummy, Naive Bayes), candidate models (Logistic Regression, Calibrated Linear SVM, Random Forest), validation threshold tuning.
4 – Backend Lead FastAPI prediction service Validated Pydantic schemas, REST endpoints (/api/predict, /api/metrics, /api/samples), and automated Pytest test suite.
5 – Frontend / Integration Lead Interactive UI & Demo Modern single-page application, real-time risk gauge, signal breakdown, model transparency dashboard, and preset demo cases.

Dataset Limitations & Ethical Considerations

  • Age of Data: The EMSCAD dataset contains postings collected primarily in the mid-2010s; modern AI-generated scam copy may exhibit linguistic subtleties not captured here.
  • Geography & Language: English-language listings dominate the dataset. Performance on non-English listings will degrade.
  • Screening Aid Only: High risk score indicates similarity to known scam patterns, not legal culpability. Users are encouraged to perform out-of-band verification.
